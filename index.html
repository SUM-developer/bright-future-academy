In this sample profile, the school brings strong teaching together with care, character and a sense of belonging, helping learners build confidence and skills.

Mission: To provide an encouraging education that helps every learner grow in knowledge, character and confidence.

Demonstration curriculum: The programmes, subjects and activities below are sample information for this fictional school.

01 / JSS 1–3

Junior Secondary

The Junior Secondary School (JSS 1–3) stage builds broad foundations as learners move into secondary education, developing literacy, numeracy, scientific inquiry, digital skills and good study habits.

Sample subjects: English Language, Mathematics, Basic Science, Basic Technology, Social Studies, Civic Education, Business Studies, Computer Studies, Nigerian Languages, Creative Arts, and Physical & Health Education.

02 / SSS 1–3

Senior Secondary

The Senior Secondary School (SSS 1–3) stage deepens subject knowledge and allows learners to focus on combinations aligned with their interests and future study.

Science: Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Further Mathematics.

Arts: Literature-in-English, Government, History, Christian Religious Studies or Islamic Studies, and languages.

Commercial: Economics, Commerce, Financial Accounting, Government and Business Studies.
